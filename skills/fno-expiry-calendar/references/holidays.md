# Trading holidays

The bundled list lives in `nsefno/data/holidays.yaml` and is copied from the exchange
circulars (NSE/FAOP/71777 of 12 Dec 2025 and NSE/FAOP/72262 of 12 Jan 2026, which added
15 Jan 2026 for the Maharashtra municipal elections). Update it every December when the
next year's circular is published, and add a test for any new expiry shifts.

2026 weekday holidays (F&O): 15 Jan, 26 Jan, 3 Mar, 26 Mar, 31 Mar, 3 Apr, 14 Apr, 1 May,
28 May, 26 Jun, 14 Sep, 2 Oct, 20 Oct, 10 Nov, 24 Nov, 25 Dec.
Muhurat trading: Sun 8 Nov 2026 (timings per exchange circular).

Expiry shifts this causes in 2026 (Tuesday expiries): 3 Mar -> 2 Mar, 31 Mar -> 30 Mar,
14 Apr -> 13 Apr, 20 Oct -> 19 Oct, 10 Nov -> 9 Nov, 24 Nov -> 23 Nov.
Thursday (BSE) shifts: 15 Jan -> 14 Jan, 26 Mar -> 25 Mar, 28 May -> 27 May.
